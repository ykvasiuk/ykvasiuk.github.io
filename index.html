I study the Universe with AI.

I'm a cosmologist at Perimeter Institute. During my Ph.D. in Moritz Münchmeyer's cosmology group at UW–Madison I developed methods for modelling and measuring the Universe — theory, computation, statistics and machine learning, applied to the cosmic microwave background and large-scale structure.

I'm equally excited about AI for science. I co-developed TPBench, one of the first benchmarks of AI reasoning in theoretical physics, and have worked on test-time scaling and on supervised and RL fine-tuning of small language models for narrow physics domains. Lately I've been exploring autoresearch: LLM-driven loops that improve algorithms on their own, from scientific codes to market-making strategies.

At Perimeter I co-organize the AI × Physics Seminar.
